# Change Of Direction And Agility Training

Change of direction (COD) is preplanned. Agility includes response to a stimulus.

## COD Components

Train:
- Deceleration
- Braking impulse
- Plant mechanics
- Trunk control
- Reacceleration
- Direction-specific footwork
- Strength qualities supporting the maneuver

## Agility Components

Train:
- Perception
- Decision-making
- Reaction to sport-specific stimuli
- Coupling perception to action
- Technical movement under uncertainty

## Drill Classification

Planned COD:
- Known route
- Good for technical development and physical capacity

Reactive agility:
- Responds to coach, opponent, ball, light, or other cue
- Good for perception-action coupling

Maneuverability:
- Changes mode, direction, or velocity in a sport context

## Progression

Progress from:
- Deceleration mechanics
- Simple planned cuts
- Multiple angles
- Higher entry speeds
- More complex sequences
- Reactive cues
- Sport-specific scenarios

## Intensity Factors

Intensity increases with:
- Higher entry speed
- Sharper angle
- More frequent cuts
- Reactive uncertainty
- Contact or opponent pressure
- Fatigue

## Programming Rules

- Teach deceleration before aggressive cutting.
- Use planned COD before reactive agility when mechanics are poor.
- Keep high-intensity COD volume controlled.
- Avoid reactive chaos when the athlete lacks basic control.

## Monitoring

Track:
- Time
- Technical quality
- Cut angle
- Entry speed
- Ground contact quality
- Athlete confidence
- Soreness and joint irritation
