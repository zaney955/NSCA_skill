# Metabolic Training Zones

Use zones to prescribe and communicate conditioning intensity.

## RPE Scale

Simple 1-10 use:
- 1-2: very easy
- 3-4: easy to moderate
- 5-6: moderate to hard
- 7-8: hard
- 9-10: very hard to maximal

RPE is useful when HR or lab data are unavailable.

## Three-Zone Model

Zone 1:
- Below first threshold
- Easy aerobic work

Zone 2:
- Between thresholds
- Moderate/threshold-oriented work

Zone 3:
- Above second threshold
- High-intensity work

## Five-Zone Model

Use when more detailed intensity control is useful:
- Zone 1: recovery/easy
- Zone 2: aerobic base
- Zone 3: tempo/threshold-adjacent
- Zone 4: hard intervals
- Zone 5: very high intensity

## Heart Rate Reserve

HRR = HRmax - HRrest.

Target HR = HRrest + intensity fraction x HRR.

Use `scripts/zone_calculator.py` when HRmax and HRrest are provided.

## Maximal Aerobic Speed

MAS can guide running interval intensity:
- Lower percentages for aerobic base and tempo
- Higher percentages for aerobic power intervals

## Work-Rest Logic

Adjust work and rest based on target:
- Aerobic base: longer work, lower intensity
- Threshold: sustained or repeated moderate-hard work
- Aerobic power: high intensity with enough rest to repeat
- Anaerobic capacity: demanding intervals, high fatigue, careful placement
- Repeated sprint ability: short high-intensity reps with incomplete or structured rest

## Safety And Environment

Outdoor conditioning must consider:
- Heat
- Cold
- Humidity
- Hydration access
- Surface
- Emergency action plan
- Athlete acclimation

Stop and refer for exertional collapse, chest pain, severe shortness of breath, confusion, suspected heat illness, or dark urine after severe exertion.
