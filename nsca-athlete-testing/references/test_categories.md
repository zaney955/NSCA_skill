# Test Categories

Use this guide to match tests to physical qualities.

## Maximum Muscular Strength

Measures low-speed maximal force capacity.

Examples:
- 1RM bench press
- 1RM back squat
- 1RM bench pull
- Isometric mid-thigh pull

Use when:
- Technique is competent
- Maximal strength informs programming
- Equipment and spotters are available

Avoid maximal dynamic tests when the athlete lacks skill or has restrictions.

## Maximum Muscular Power

Measures high-speed force expression.

Examples:
- Vertical jump
- Standing long jump
- Squat jump
- Drop jump
- Rebound jump
- Power clean

Interpret power tests with body mass context when relevant.

## Anaerobic Capacity

Measures high-intensity effort over moderate durations.

Examples:
- Shuttle runs
- Repeated box jump tests
- Cycle ergometer tests

These tests are fatiguing and should be placed late in a session.

## Local Muscular Endurance

Measures repeated contractions against submaximal resistance.

Examples:
- Push-up test
- Partial curl-up
- Fixed-load bench press repetitions

Standardize range of motion and stopping criteria.

## Aerobic Capacity

Estimates ability to sustain oxidative energy production.

Examples:
- Timed distance runs
- Fixed-time runs
- Intermittent recovery tests
- Maximal aerobic speed tests

Choose based on sport type and facility constraints.

## Speed

Measures time over a fixed distance.

Examples:
- 10 m or 10 yd acceleration
- 20 m or 20 yd sprint
- 40 yd sprint

Electronic timing is preferred. Splits are more informative than a single final time.

## Change Of Direction And Agility

Change of direction tests involve preplanned movement. Agility tests include a perceptual-cognitive response to a stimulus.

Examples:
- T-test
- 5-10-5
- 5-0-5
- Hexagon test
- Illinois test

Do not call a preplanned COD test a complete agility test.

## Mobility, Flexibility, Balance, And Movement Competency

Examples:
- Sit-and-reach
- Overhead squat screen
- Balance error scoring
- Star excursion balance

Use these as training information, not definitive injury prediction.

## Body Composition And Anthropometry

Examples:
- Body mass
- Height
- Girths
- Skinfolds
- Bioelectrical impedance

Use trained testers, consistent timing, and appropriate privacy. Be careful with body composition communication, especially with youth or athletes at risk for disordered eating.
