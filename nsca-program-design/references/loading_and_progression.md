# Loading And Progression

## Loading Inputs

Preferred order:
1. Recent valid 1RM
2. Multiple-RM set with good technique
3. Recent training load and RPE/RIR
4. Conservative estimate from experience

Avoid high-load testing for beginners, fatigued athletes, injured athletes, and athletes without technique competence.

## Estimated 1RM

Common estimates are useful for planning, but they are not exact. Accuracy decreases as repetition count rises. Prefer estimates from <= 10 reps.

Use `scripts/estimate_1rm.py` when load and reps are supplied.

## Percent-Based Loading

Use when:
- The 1RM is recent and valid
- Exercise technique is stable
- The athlete is not unusually fatigued

Adjust when:
- In-season fatigue is high
- Body mass changes significantly
- Technique or range of motion changed
- Athlete reports poor readiness

## RPE/RIR Loading

Use RPE/RIR when:
- No valid 1RM exists
- Daily readiness fluctuates
- The athlete is in-season
- The goal is autoregulation

Simple mapping:
- RPE 6: easy, several reps in reserve
- RPE 7: moderate, about 3 reps in reserve
- RPE 8: hard, about 2 reps in reserve
- RPE 9: very hard, about 1 rep in reserve
- RPE 10: maximal, no reps in reserve

## Progression Rules

Load increase may be appropriate when:
- All prescribed reps are completed
- Technique is stable
- RPE is lower than target
- Recovery is acceptable
- The athlete is not near a key competition

Conservative increases:
- Upper body: 2.5%-5%
- Lower body: 5%-10%
- Power exercises: smaller jumps, preserve speed and technique
- Novices: increase only when technique quality is consistent

## Volume-Load

Volume-load = sets x reps x load.

Use it to compare workload across sessions or weeks. Do not use it as the only measure of stress because exercise type, range of motion, velocity, and proximity to failure matter.

## Deloads

Use deloads when:
- A mesocycle accumulates fatigue
- Testing or competition is near
- Performance decreases for repeated sessions
- The athlete has high nontraining stress

Common deload strategy:
- Reduce volume more than intensity
- Maintain skill and intensity exposure
- Remove high-risk accessory fatigue

## Output Requirement

When prescribing loads, state:
- Basis: 1RM, e1RM, RPE/RIR, or technical quality
- Target range
- Progression rule
- Regression rule
