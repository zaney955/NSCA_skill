# Population Considerations

Use these rules to scale training conservatively.

## Youth Athletes

Priorities:
- Technique competence
- Fundamental movement skills
- Supervision
- Gradual progression
- Enjoyment and consistency

Avoid:
- Maximal testing before skill readiness
- Excessive fatigue
- Adult programs copied without scaling
- High-volume plyometrics or heavy loads without preparation

## Older Adults

Priorities:
- Medical clearance when risk factors exist
- Joint-friendly exercise selection
- Balance, power, strength, and daily function
- Longer recovery between hard sessions
- Conservative progression

Avoid:
- Breath-holding emphasis for those with cardiovascular concerns
- Rapid progression
- Complex high-risk exercises without skill preparation

## Female Athletes

Do not assume lower trainability. Consider:
- Individual strength and training history
- Menstrual cycle symptoms when the athlete reports relevant effects
- Energy availability concerns
- Landing mechanics, knee control, and hip strength when sport demands warrant

## Beginners

Priorities:
- Full-body routines
- Technique mastery
- Moderate volume
- Conservative loads
- Simple progression

## Intermediate Athletes

Priorities:
- More targeted exercise selection
- Clear block goals
- Progressive overload
- Moderate variation

## Advanced Athletes

Priorities:
- Greater individualization
- More precise loading
- More variation
- Fatigue management
- Monitoring and planned deloads

## In-Season Athletes

Priorities:
- Maintain strength and power
- Reduce volume
- Minimize soreness
- Place hard training away from competitions
- Use readiness-based adjustments
