# Resistance Exercise Checklists

These are pattern-based checklists. Adapt to the specific exercise, athlete, and equipment.

## Squat Pattern

Setup:
- Feet stable and appropriate width
- Bar or implement positioned securely
- Trunk braced
- Eyes and head neutral

Execution:
- Descend under control
- Knees track with toes
- Maintain foot pressure
- Keep trunk position appropriate to variation
- Drive up without losing alignment

Common issues:
- Knees collapse inward
- Heels rise
- Excessive trunk collapse
- Depth beyond control
- Uneven weight shift

## Hinge Pattern

Setup:
- Feet stable
- Hips initiate movement
- Spine position controlled
- Lats engaged when holding a bar

Execution:
- Push hips back
- Keep implement close when relevant
- Maintain brace
- Finish with hip extension, not lumbar overextension

Common issues:
- Squatting the hinge
- Rounding under load
- Bar drifting forward
- Overextending at finish

## Horizontal Push

Setup:
- Stable body contact
- Grip balanced
- Shoulder blades controlled
- Feet grounded when applicable

Execution:
- Lower with control
- Keep wrists stacked
- Press evenly
- Finish without losing shoulder position

Common issues:
- Bouncing
- Uneven lockout
- Excessive arch changes
- Elbows flaring beyond control

## Horizontal Pull

Setup:
- Torso or chest supported when applicable
- Grip symmetrical
- Shoulders set

Execution:
- Pull with elbows moving appropriately
- Control end range
- Lower under control
- Avoid excessive trunk motion unless intended

Common issues:
- Shrugging
- Jerking
- Losing torso position
- Incomplete ROM

## Vertical Push

Setup:
- Stable stance or seated contact
- Brace
- Grip balanced
- Bar path clear

Execution:
- Press overhead with control
- Keep ribs and pelvis controlled
- Finish with stable lockout

Common issues:
- Excessive lumbar extension
- Forward bar path
- Uneven press
- Poor overhead mobility compensated by spine

## Vertical Pull

Setup:
- Grip appropriate
- Trunk controlled
- Shoulders prepared

Execution:
- Pull without excessive swinging
- Reach intended end range
- Control return

Common issues:
- Kipping when not intended
- Forward head
- Incomplete range
- Excessive shrugging

## Single-Leg Pattern

Setup:
- Stable starting stance
- Pelvis controlled
- Knee and foot aligned

Execution:
- Move slowly enough to control alignment
- Keep knee tracking with foot
- Use full controlled range

Common issues:
- Hip drop
- Knee valgus
- Foot collapse
- Rushing the movement

## Power Exercise Pattern

Setup:
- Qualified supervision
- Clear area
- Appropriate platform
- Light technical loading first

Execution:
- Explosive intent
- Strong positions
- No grinding
- Stop before fatigue degrades technique

Common issues:
- Pulling with arms early
- Poor receiving position
- Jumping forward
- Fatigue-based technique breakdown
