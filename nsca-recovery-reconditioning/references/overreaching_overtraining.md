# Overreaching And Overtraining

## Key Distinctions

Normal fatigue:
- Expected short-term tiredness after training
- Resolves with normal recovery
- Performance rebounds quickly

Functional overreaching:
- Planned short-term performance decrement
- Followed by recovery and performance improvement
- Requires deliberate unloading

Nonfunctional overreaching:
- Performance decrement lasts longer than intended
- Recovery may take weeks
- Often includes mood, sleep, soreness, or motivation issues

Overtraining syndrome concern:
- Long-term performance decrement
- May involve physiological and psychological maladaptation
- Recovery may take weeks to months
- Requires qualified professional evaluation

## Underperformance Is Not Always Overtraining

Consider:
- Insufficient sleep
- Illness
- Travel
- Poor fueling or hydration
- Psychological stress
- Injury
- Testing inconsistency
- Competition schedule
- Life stress
- Technical changes
- Medication or medical conditions

## Risk Factors

Training factors:
- Excessive volume
- Excessive intensity
- Monotony
- Insufficient deloads
- Too much novelty
- Dense competition

Nontraining factors:
- Poor sleep
- High stress
- Inadequate energy intake
- Illness
- Travel/time zones
- Family/work/school demands

## Warning Signs

Performance:
- Repeated decrease in strength, power, speed, or endurance
- Slower recovery between sessions
- Higher RPE for normal loads

Subjective:
- Persistent fatigue
- Poor sleep
- Unusual soreness
- Low motivation
- Mood changes
- Irritability

Physiological:
- Abnormal resting heart rate trend
- Appetite changes
- Frequent illness

These signs are not diagnostic. They are reasons to adjust training and consider referral.

## Practical Response

Low concern:
- Continue monitoring
- Keep planned recovery

Moderate concern:
- Reduce volume
- Keep technique and light intensity exposure
- Increase recovery support
- Reassess in several days

High concern:
- Stop hard training
- Involve coach and medical/allied health staff
- Review nontraining stressors
- Do not test maximally
