# Pre, During, And Post Event Nutrition

## Pre-Event

Goals:
- Arrive fueled
- Support hydration
- Avoid GI distress
- Match meal timing to event time

Consider:
- Event duration and intensity
- Athlete tolerance
- Familiar foods
- Carbohydrate availability
- Fluid access

Avoid unfamiliar foods or aggressive changes near competition.

## During Event

Needed more often when:
- Event is long
- Training is prolonged
- Multiple events occur in one day
- Environment is hot
- Glycogen depletion risk is high

Consider:
- Carbohydrate intake
- Fluid intake
- Electrolytes
- GI tolerance
- Practical access

## Post-Event Or Post-Training

Goals:
- Rehydrate
- Restore carbohydrate when needed
- Provide protein for repair/adaptation
- Replace electrolytes when sweat losses are high
- Support next-session readiness

When next session is soon, recovery nutrition becomes more time-sensitive.

## Competition Day Planning

Plan:
- Meal timing
- Portable foods
- Fluid availability
- Backup options
- GI-safe choices
- Recovery between events

## Monitoring

Track:
- Energy during session
- GI comfort
- Cramping or dizziness
- Urine color as a rough hydration clue
- Body mass changes when appropriate
- Performance and recovery
