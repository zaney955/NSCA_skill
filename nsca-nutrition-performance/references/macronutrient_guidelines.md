# Macronutrient Guidelines

Use broad ranges and adjust by training load, body size, goals, and tolerance. These are not medical prescriptions.

## Carbohydrate

Purpose:
- Supports high-intensity training
- Supports glycogen stores
- Supports recovery between sessions

Higher needs when:
- Training volume is high
- Multiple sessions per day
- Endurance or repeated sprint demands
- Competition is long or dense

Lower needs may fit:
- Rest days
- Low-intensity days
- Lower-volume blocks

## Protein

Purpose:
- Supports repair and adaptation
- Helps preserve lean mass
- Supports recovery

Practical approach:
- Distribute protein across meals
- Include protein after training when recovery window matters
- Pair with carbohydrate when glycogen restoration is also important

## Fat

Purpose:
- Energy
- Hormonal and cellular function
- Fat-soluble nutrient support

Avoid extremely low-fat patterns unless supervised by a qualified professional.

## Energy Availability

Insufficient energy can impair:
- Performance
- Recovery
- Mood
- Menstrual function
- Bone health
- Immune function

Persistent underfueling or RED-S concern requires qualified evaluation.

## Macro Estimate Use

Use `scripts/macro_estimator.py` for broad gram-per-kilogram ranges. Treat ranges as planning starts; adjust with athlete feedback, performance, body mass trends, and qualified nutrition support.
