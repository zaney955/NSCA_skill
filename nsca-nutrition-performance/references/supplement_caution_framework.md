# Supplement Caution Framework

Supplements can carry efficacy, safety, contamination, and eligibility risks.

## First Questions

Ask:
- What is the goal?
- What product and dose?
- What ingredients?
- Is the athlete drug tested?
- What governing body rules apply?
- Is the product third-party tested?
- Does the athlete have medical conditions or medications?
- Is there a qualified dietitian or physician involved?

## Risk Categories

Evidence risk:
- Claims may exceed evidence
- Benefits may be context-specific

Safety risk:
- Side effects
- Interactions
- Excessive dosing
- Unknown long-term use

Contamination risk:
- Undeclared banned substances
- Manufacturing variability

Compliance risk:
- Rules change
- Banned lists may not be exhaustive
- Responsibility often remains with the athlete

## Third-Party Testing

Third-party testing can reduce risk but does not make a product risk-free. Encourage athletes to use recognized testing resources and consult governing-body compliance staff.

## Response Rule

Do not say a supplement is safe or permitted. Provide a risk checklist and recommend qualified review.

## Referral

Refer when:
- Athlete is drug tested
- Product has hormone-like claims
- Product has stimulant claims
- Athlete has medical conditions
- Athlete is a minor
- Dose exceeds label or common use
