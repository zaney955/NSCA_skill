# Hydration And Electrolytes

## Hydration Goals

Support:
- Performance
- Thermoregulation
- Recovery
- Cognitive function

## Individualization

Hydration needs vary with:
- Sweat rate
- Heat and humidity
- Altitude
- Exercise duration and intensity
- Clothing/equipment
- Body size
- Acclimation

## Practical Markers

Useful but imperfect:
- Thirst
- Urine color
- Body mass change across session
- Sweat rate estimates
- Performance and perceived exertion

## Electrolytes

Sodium may matter more when:
- Sweat losses are high
- Sessions are long
- Heat exposure is high
- Multiple sessions occur in a day
- Athlete is a salty sweater

Do not recommend aggressive electrolyte or fluid intake without context. Overhydration can be dangerous.

## Environmental Risk

Heat, humidity, and heavy equipment increase risk. For suspected heat illness, confusion, collapse, severe weakness, or abnormal symptoms, stop activity and seek immediate medical help.

## Plan Elements

Include:
- Pre-session hydration habit
- During-session access
- Post-session rehydration
- Electrolyte strategy when justified
- Monitoring and adjustment
